QUALITRACK QMS 9 -- FIELD REFERENCE (FICTIONAL VENDOR DOCUMENTATION FOR TRAINING USE ONLY)

Module: Deviation Management
- source_case_id: System-generated identifier of a deviation record. Format DEV-YYYY-NNNN. Immutable once created.
- event_code: Deviation category. DEV-EQP equipment malfunction or alarm; DEV-DOC documentation or GDP error;
  DEV-ENV environmental monitoring excursion; DEV-PROC process parameter outside normal operating range;
  DEV-LAB laboratory or sample handling issue.
- status_code: Workflow state. OPEN (logged, triage pending); IN_INVESTIGATION (root cause analysis underway);
  PENDING_CAPA (investigation complete, corrective actions open); CLOSED (QA approved closure); CANCELLED (raised in error).
- reported_at: Date and time the deviation was first recorded by the originator.
- target_due_at: Due date for closure, by default 30 calendar days after reported_at per site procedure.
- resolved_at: Date and time QA approved closure or cancellation.
- severity: Risk classification introduced in release 9.2. MINOR (no product impact), MAJOR (potential product impact,
  requires impact assessment), CRITICAL (probable product impact, requires immediate escalation).
- lot_id: Affected manufacturing lot when the deviation is lot specific; empty for facility-level deviations.

Module: CAPA
- source_action_id: Identifier of a corrective or preventive action, format CA-NNNNNN.
- action_code: CONTAINMENT (immediate control), CORRECTIVE (removes the root cause), PREVENTIVE (prevents recurrence
  elsewhere), EFFECTIVENESS_CHECK (verifies the action worked).
- action_status: OPEN, IN_PROGRESS, COMPLETED, CANCELLED.
- assigned_group: Functional group accountable for the action.

Module: Change Control
- source_change_id: Identifier of a change control record, format CC-YYYY-NNN.
- change_type: EQUIPMENT, PROCESS, DOCUMENT, SUPPLIER, ANALYTICAL_METHOD. EQUIPMENT, PROCESS and ANALYTICAL_METHOD
  changes are product-quality impacting and require QA approval before implementation.
- workflow_status: SUBMITTED, APPROVED, IMPLEMENTED, CLOSED, REJECTED.
- effective_at: Date the change was implemented in the GMP environment.

LIMS interface (QualiTrack Lab Connector)
- test_code: TITER_G_L product titer in g/L; SEC_MONOMER_PCT size-exclusion purity, percent monomer;
  ENDOTOXIN_EU_ML bacterial endotoxin in EU/mL; BIOBURDEN_CFU colony forming units per 10 mL; PH; HCP_PPM host cell protein in ppm.
- measured_value: Reported numeric result. Results below the limit of quantitation are transmitted as the text '<LOQ'.
- lower_limit / upper_limit: Specification limits valid for the product at the time of testing.
- A result outside [lower_limit, upper_limit] is an Out-Of-Specification (OOS) result and must be investigated.
